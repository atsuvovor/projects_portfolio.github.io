Fraud Detection System

Overview

The Fraud Detection System uses data-driven methods to identify and mitigate suspicious transactions in real time.

Model Design

Machine learning models are trained on large transactional datasets to identify abnormal spending behaviors and potential fraud attempts.

Data Sources

The system aggregates and analyzes transaction data, customer behavior, and risk indicators from various internal and external sources.

Monitoring & Reporting

Continuous model performance tracking ensures the fraud detection framework remains accurate and adaptable to new threat patterns.
